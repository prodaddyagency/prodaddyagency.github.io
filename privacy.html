Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ProDaddy Agency ("we", "us") is a video editing and digital marketing business registered in India (MSME / Udyam). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, email address, phone number and business name.
  • Files you share with us for a project, such as videos, photos, logos and brand guidelines.
  • Basic, anonymous website usage data that your browser shares, such as device type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for.
  • To send quotes, invoices and project updates.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Your files and content

Footage and materials you send stay yours. We use them only to work on your project. We will not show your project in our portfolio unless you give us written permission.

Sharing

We share information only with the tools we need to deliver the work (for example email, cloud storage and payment providers), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project files for up to 90 days after delivery so we can handle revisions, then delete them unless you ask us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email hello@prodaddyagency.com.
