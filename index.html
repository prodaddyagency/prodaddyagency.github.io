FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where are you based?

ProDaddy Agency is based in Andhra Pradesh, India. We work with businesses in India, Australia and Canada, fully online.

What do you need from me?

Your raw clips (phone footage is fine), your logo and brand colours, and any example video you like. We can also use licensed stock footage.

How fast will I get my video?

Most single reels are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Because of the time difference, footage sent in your evening is usually ready by your morning.

How do I pay?

International clients can pay by card or bank transfer through our invoice, or order through our Fiverr and Upwork profiles. Indian clients can pay by UPI or bank transfer.

Can I get a free sample first?

Yes. Send us one short clip and we will edit the first 10 to 15 seconds for free, so you can see our style before you pay.
